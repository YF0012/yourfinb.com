The numbers we keep an eye on

Six measures that show how healthy your business really is, explained in plain English. Figures in the graphics are examples.

Cash runway

How many months your cash will last at today’s spending.

Why it matters: It tells you when to raise, cut or invest, before it is urgent.

Gross margin

What you keep from each sale after direct costs.

Why it matters: It shows whether growth is actually making you money.

Burn rate

How much cash the business uses each month.

Why it matters: Small savings here add months to your runway.

Revenue growth

How fast sales are rising, month by month.

Why it matters: Investors and lenders look here first.

Days to get paid

The time between sending an invoice and cash arriving.

Why it matters: Faster collection frees up cash without new funding.

Budget vs actual

Where you landed against the plan.

Why it matters: Spot overspending early and adjust the forecast.

    Getting started

    How do we get started?

    With a free discovery call. We learn about your business, your current books and what you need from your numbers, then send a simple plan and scope.

    What do you need from us to begin?

    Access to your accounting system and bank feeds, recent financial reports if you have them, and one person on your side who can answer questions.

    How long does setup take?

    It depends on how up to date your books are. If records are behind, we tidy them first. We agree a clear timeline with you before we start.

    Do you work with businesses outside the US?

    Yes. We are a fully remote team and work with clients in many countries. We agree regular meeting times that work for both sides.

    Is the first consultation really free?

    Yes. The discovery call is free and there is no obligation to go ahead.

    Our services

    Can I combine services?

    Yes. Most clients start with bookkeeping or FP&A and add Virtual CFO support or fundraising work as they grow.

    What is the difference between bookkeeping and a Virtual CFO?

    Bookkeeping keeps your records accurate and up to date. A Virtual CFO uses those numbers to plan cash, guide decisions and work with your board, lenders and investors.

    Do we need a Virtual CFO or a full-time CFO?

    A Virtual CFO gives you senior finance support part-time, so you pay only for the time you need. Many businesses move to a full-time CFO later, and we can help with that handover.

    Our books are behind. Can you catch them up?

    Yes. Bringing past records up to date is part of setup, so your monthly reports start from a clean base.

    Can you help us prepare to raise money?

    Yes. We build the financial model, investor reporting pack and cap table, and support you through due diligence.

    Do you handle tax filing?

    Our focus is bookkeeping, planning and reporting. We keep your books ready for year-end and work alongside your tax adviser.

    Working together

    Which accounting software do you use?

    We work in the accounting system you already use, so there is no migration project before you see value.

    What reports will we receive?

    Monthly management reports with your profit and loss, balance sheet and cash position, plus budget against actual when we run your FP&A.

    How often will we meet?

    We agree a regular rhythm with you, usually a monthly review of your numbers, with extra calls when decisions come up.

    How do we communicate day to day?

    By email and scheduled video calls, with one named contact on our side who knows your business.

    Who has access to our financial data?

    We work inside your own systems with access you grant and can remove at any time. Your books stay yours.

    Pricing and commitment

    How much do your services cost?

    Pricing depends on the services you choose, your transaction volume and how complex your business is. We send a clear quote after the discovery call.

    Can we change our services later?

    Yes. Your scope can grow or shrink as your needs change. We review it with you regularly.

    What if we want to stop?

    Notice terms are set out in your engagement letter. If you leave, we hand over your records and reports in good order.
